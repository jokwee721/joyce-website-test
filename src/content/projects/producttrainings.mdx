## Problem

Traditional, synchronous onboarding and product training sessions place a burden on the training team, leading to inconsistent learner knowledge retention in the product line.

## Solution

Designed and developed a scalable, multi-part e-learning experience tailored for 500+ internal employees across diverse organizational functions. By integrating interactive scenario-based branching in Articulate Suite, modern visual assets via Canva, and rapid asset generation using Generative AI image pipelines, the learning experience transformed complex product architecture into modular, engaging digital modules that learners could complete asynchronously with high fidelity and zero direct trainer intervention.

1. **Script Writing & Storyboarding:** Translated dense technical product architecture into clear, role-relevant narratives and structured multi-part storyboards tailored for cross-departmental learners.
2. **Visual Design & Generative AI Asset Pipeline:** Paired Canva layout systems with custom Generative AI visual workflows to accelerate high-fidelity UI and product illustration production.
3. **Interactive Prototype Development:** Built scenario-based branching interactions in Articulate Suite to enable self-paced, asynchronous mastery—eliminating 30 hours of repetitive live facilitation.

## End-to-End Development Phases

* **Phase 1 — Analysis (Instructional Needs Analysis & Cognitive Task Mapping · September 2026):**
  * Collaborated with cross-functional subject matter experts across R&D, engineering, and manufacturing to scope core competencies and technical constraints.
  * Applied adult learning principles (Knowles' Andragogy) and multimedia learning theory (Mayer's Cognitive Theory) to structure modular units.
* **Phase 2 — Design (Scriptwriting & Rapid Storyboarding · September – October 2026):**
  * Authored human-centric, scenario-driven branching scripts mirroring real-world operational friction points for technical and non-technical staff.
  * Developed rapid low-to-high fidelity storyboards mapping UI interactions, decision branch nodes, and knowledge checks.
* **Phase 3 — Development (Visual Design & Generative Asset Pipeline · October 2026):**
  * Established a cohesive visual design system utilizing Canva for clean UI components, custom infographics, and iconography.
  * Engineered a Generative AI image workflow to produce contextualized, brand-aligned visual assets and product mockups dynamically.
* **Phase 4 — Prototyping & Iterative Testing:**
  * Developed functional interactive prototypes in the Articulate Suite with advanced variables and conditional branching.
  * Completed thorough QA testing across every slide—verifying spelling, grammar, interactive triggers, and WCAG accessibility standards (POUR: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust).
* **Phase 5 — Implementation & Impact:**
  * Deployed the final multi-part series across the enterprise learning ecosystem, tracking completion rates and interaction metrics.
  * Streamlined the onboarding pipeline, achieving a measurable reduction of 30 instructional hours for the training team.
