## Overview

The COVID-19 pandemic negatively impacted children and teens' psychological well-being and was linked to challenging behaviors—including harm to self, harm to others, poor communication, and classroom disruptions during lockdown and beyond. **Empower** is a mobile app created for parents and teachers to facilitate connection to actionable behavioral resources. During this project, I served as the sole UX Researcher and Designer from start to finish, meeting weekly with a design mentor.

## Research & Core Insights

* **“Problematic behaviors are a form of communication.”** Research revealed that educators and parents understood students were trying to communicate an underlying need, but felt overwhelmed by where to start or how to pinpoint that need.
* **“Problematic behaviors affect me.”** Users needed a centralized, trustworthy resource providing immediate support when challenging behaviors arose across home and classroom settings.
* Synthesized findings into two primary user personas: **Persona 1 (Educator)** and **Persona 2 (Parent)**.

## Wireframes, Mockups & Style Guide

Designed the core user flow for discovering, bookmarking, and organizing evidence-based behavioral articles:
`Home Screen → Article → Save Article → Profile → Retrieve Folder → Open Folder`.

## Takeaways

This was my first time leading a UX design project end-to-end while wearing multiple hats as both UX Researcher and UI Designer. Presenting my research, wireframes, style guide, and interactive prototype demonstrated the tangible impact user-centered research has on product architecture.
