## Project Overview

In this project, I addressed the challenge of learner passivity by transforming the theoretical ADDIE framework (Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation) into an interactive, application-based microlesson. By simulating a real-world digital environment, I reduced the barrier between "learning" the model and "using" the model.

## Process & Artifacts

* **Design Document:** In this stage, I developed the project overview, researched the target audience, and drafted the prerequisite knowledge, project objectives, and content overview.
* **Storyboard:** I planned the screens and included detailed design notes on where text and interactions would logistically flow. For learners to complete the process successfully, breaking down the flow into 8 steps made the most sense. This is also the stage where I wrote out the assessment questions and targeted remediation feedback for correct and incorrect responses.
* **Mind Map:** I started with the core problem and broke up the sections that would best help novice instructional designers understand the ADDIE model—including the introduction, navigation tutorial, definitions, drag-and-drop activity, and knowledge test.
* **Development in Lectora:** Serving as both SME and instructional designer, I built the end-to-end project in Lectora. Transitioning from Articulate Storyline's slide-based "Story View" to Lectora's hierarchical folder-based Project Explorer deepened my versatility across authoring platforms, particularly in organizing triggers, variables, and WCAG-compliant states.
